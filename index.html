About the Symposium

The International Symposium on Interdisciplinary Ecology brings together researchers, practitioners, and students from diverse biological and environmental sciences to foster cross-disciplinary collaboration.

We welcome submissions on biodiversity, global change, evolutionary ecology, and ecosystem management. Join us for three days of impactful talks, poster sessions, and networking.

Call for Abstracts: Deadline September 1, 2026. Submit your proposals to abstracts@isie2026.org.

Scientific Themes

01

Climate-Change Biology

Understanding range shifts, thermal adaptation, and ecological impacts under global climate warming.

02

Evolutionary Genomics

Exploring genomic mechanisms underlying adaptation, speciation, and complex ecological interactions.

03

Community & Ecosystems

Dissecting species interactions, food webs, and the ecosystem processes that sustain biodiversity.

04

Behavioral Ecology

Analyzing decision-making, social structures, and adaptive behaviors across varied environmental gradients.
